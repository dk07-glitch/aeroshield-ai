# 🛡️ SaafSaans (AeroShield AI) | Winter Smog & Urban Clean Air Navigator

**SaafSaans** (*"Clean Breath"*) is a specialized GenAI assistant and interactive air quality platform built to empower families, vulnerable populations, and local communities to navigate severe winter smog, hazardous atmospheric temperature inversions, and urban particulate spikes ($\text{PM}_{2.5}$, $\text{PM}_{10}$, $\text{NO}_2$, $\text{SO}_2$, $\text{CO}$).

The platform combines real-time atmospheric boundary physics modeling, indoor air quality (IAQ) engineering hacks, personalized low-emission daily routines, and quantifiable impact tracking.

---

## 🌟 Core Highlights & Features

### 1. 📡 Smog Radar & Atmospheric Inversion Simulator
- **Thermal Inversion Modeling:** Visualizes why winter cold air traps ground-level emissions under a stagnant warm atmospheric cap.
- **Interactive Boundary Height Slider:** Dynamically simulate the boundary layer from 100m (heavy stagnation) up to 600m (clean solar convective dispersal).
- **Dual-State Particle Canvas:** Live interactive comparison of outdoor toxic aerosols ($295\ \mu\text{g/m}^3$) versus an indoor purified microclimate ($<12\ \mu\text{g/m}^3$).
- **WHO & Berkeley Earth Benchmark:** Computes real-time inhalation burden in equivalent cigarettes avoided ($22\ \mu\text{g/m}^3 \approx 1\text{ cigarette/day}$).

### 2. 🧠 "Aura" GenAI Clean Air Copilot
- **Context-Aware Reasoning Engine:** Adapts responses based on the active city, current stagnation index, and household vulnerability profile.
- **Pre-Built Intelligence Chips:**
  - *Child & Elder Day Planner*
  - *DIY Corsi-Rosenthal Box Sizing & Physics*
  - *Midday Solar Ventilation Window*
  - *Cardiovascular Exercise & Outdoor Exertion Risk*
- **Peer-Reviewed Guidance:** Integrated citations from WHO IAQ Guidelines, The Lancet Planetary Health, and ASHRAE.

### 3. 📅 24-Hour Adaptive Smog Daily Schedule
- Synchronized with the planetary boundary layer diurnal cycle:
  - **06:30 – 08:00 (Morning Peak):** Air sanctuary lockdown, airway hydration, peak flow tracking.
  - **08:00 – 09:30 (Rush-Hour Surge):** Low-diesel routes, vehicle AC recirculation mode, N95 perimeter seal.
  - **09:30 – 13:00 (Deep Work):** Wet microfiber dust suppression, low-speed HEPA filtration.
  - **13:00 – 15:30 (🌟 Solar Ventilation Slot):** Synchronized 15-minute cross-ventilation flush to clear indoor $\text{CO}_2$ ($>1,200\text{ ppm}$) without soot infiltration.
  - **16:00 – 18:30 (Evening Seal-Down):** Induction cooking, exhaust hood activation, window perimeter latching.
  - **19:00 – 22:30 (Night Sanctuary):** 45% relative humidity regulation for particulate settling.
- **Daily Shield Protection Score:** Interactive task completion tracker updating personal airway defense percentage.

### 4. 🛠️ IAQ Lab & DIY Corsi-Rosenthal Box Sizing Calculator
- **Interactive Engineering Simulator:** Select room square footage, ceiling height, fan speed, and filter depth (1" vs 2" MERV-13) to calculate:
  - Clean Air Delivery Rate (CADR in CFM)
  - Air Changes Per Hour (ACH)
  - Minutes to 90% particle clearance
  - Cost savings compared to commercial HEPA brands
- **Peer-Reviewed Hack Blueprints:**
  - Inversion ventilation timing ($0 cost, 70% particle reduction)
  - Thermal silicone weatherstripping ($8–$15 cost, 65% infiltration reduction)
  - Induction cooking protocols (eliminating indoor $\text{NO}_2$ and unburned methane)
  - 45% relative humidity particulate trap
  - NASA phytoremediation arrays

### 5. 🌱 Sustainable Alternatives Ledger
- Side-by-side comparative ledger contrasting conventional high-polluting habits with sustainable solutions:
  - **Heating:** Kerosene & wood stoves $\rightarrow$ Split inverter heat pump & mica-thermic radiant heat.
  - **Commute:** Single fossil car $\rightarrow$ Rail transit + clean EV shuttle.
  - **Garden Waste:** Open leaf burning $\rightarrow$ Municipal Bokashi mulching.
  - **Cooking:** Open gas flames $\rightarrow$ Sealed induction + convection.
  - **School Gates:** Idling diesel SUVs $\rightarrow$ Walking buses & 100m zero-idling perimeters.

### 6. 📊 Direct Impact Calculator & Certified Guardian Awards
- Quantifies real-time household contributions:
  - Grams of $\text{PM}_{2.5}$ averted from direct inhalation.
  - Kilograms of $\text{CO}_2\text{e}$ greenhouse emissions prevented.
  - Cigarette smoke equivalent avoided.
  - Financial savings in fuel, medical care, and DIY filter efficiency.
- **Confetti Celebration & Clean Air Guardian Certificate:** Generates shareable recognition cards for community mobilization.

### 7. 🤝 Community Mutual Aid & Burning Hotspot Hub
- Group purchase drives for bulk MERV-13 filters at 45% wholesale discounts.
- School walking buses and GPS-logged citizen reporting for illegal open burning or toxic plumes.
- Built-in **Soothing Binaural Ambient Sound** (Web Audio rain/air synthesizer) in the navigation bar to ease acute respiratory anxiety during hazardous smog episodes.

## 🚀 Getting Started

### Prerequisites
- Node.js (v18+)
- npm or yarn

### Installation
```bash
# Clone the repository
git clone https://github.com/dk07-glitch/aeroshield-ai.git
cd aeroshield-ai

# Install dependencies
npm install

# Start development server
npm run dev

# Build for production
npm run build
```
